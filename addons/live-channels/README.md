# Live Channels

A lightweight D-pad launcher for your TV's live video sources: HDMI/AV ports, the TV tuner, and installed broadcast or IPTV apps.

This add-on plugs into Clean Your TV as a **module card** (`Live Channels`).

## What it does

- **HDMI & AV Inputs**: Launches TV inputs directly using standard Android TV passthrough channel URIs (`TvContract`).
- **TV Tuner**: Direct shortcut to your TV's hardware tuner.
- **Installed Broadcast Apps**: Direct launcher for installed live TV, IPTV, or streaming broadcast apps.
- **Management**: Add, rename, reorder (Move Up / Down), and delete sources.
- **Open Last Source**: Remembers your last used input or channel app for immediate 1-click access.
- **Open on launch**: Optional toggle to directly switch to your last live source when Live Channels is launched.

> [!NOTE]
> Live Channels launches video sources and apps that already exist on your TV. It does **not** include, distribute, or stream any playlists, channel lists, or M3U/HLS stream URLs.

## Permissions

None. Live Channels requests zero Android permissions (`INTERNET`, background services, and special permissions are completely omitted).

## Tested on

- Mi TV 4A (Android TV 9, Pie)
